


RBAC SCOPE
- City Scope: Controls operational visibility for drivers, customers, bookings, and staff across cities.
- Organization Scope: Represents the legal/billing entity responsible for invoices and financial records.
- RydCare Staff Scope: Staff access is assigned city-wise by default, with authorized cross-city access for support.
- Driver Scope: Drivers have a primary city, permitted work types, and optional client-branch eligibility.
- Customer Scope: General-public customers and their bookings are associated with the relevant service city.
- Client Scope: Client users can be granted access across all permitted branches belonging to their client.
- Client Branch Scope: Restricts client users to specific branches and supports branch-level operations and tariffs.
- Cross-City Support: Authorized staff can assist other cities without changing the original ownership of bookings or financial records.
