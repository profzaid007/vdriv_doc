## Rydcare Business Model

### 1) Value Proposition:

- Driver Demand Fulfillment
- Driver Operations as service
- To provide seamless one stop driver mobility solutions
- Professionalize the Driver Ecosystem
- Better standard of pay

Reliable managed driver services without clients having to recruit, manage and coordinate their own driver workforce

Demand → Driver → Assignment → Trip → Tracking → Completion → Billing → Payment → Reporting

### 2) Customer Segment:

Current types of customers (B2B) and their needs:

- Automobile Service centers / Showrooms (Primary)
    - Vehicle pickup/drop for service-center operation
    - Vehicle RTO registration 
    - Vehicle Delivery Drop

- Hotels , Marriage Halls and Hospitality Centre (Secondary)
    - Valet - Monthly Drivers
    - Valet - Acting Drivers

Future Addition : End customers / General Public (Round-trip, Incity, Only Drop , Outstation)  - Acting Drivers

Corporate clients:
- WhatsApp Groups / Chat and Phone support for Daily Booking coordination, Driver allocation
- Accident Resolution
- Monthly billing / Invoice doubt clearing

Drivers:
- Phone and Whatsapp Support - Unmonitored 

End Customers:
- Phone and Whatsapp Support

Immediate next set of needed actions:

- Client Onboarding - Tariffs -> Quotations -> Confirmation -> Agreement Signing
- Client-specific SOPs

- Regular operational reviews and Feedback
